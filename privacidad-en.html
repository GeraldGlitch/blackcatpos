Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Overview

BlackCatPOS is 100% free point-of-sale software designed to keep your data yours. Your business information (sales, inventory, customers, employees, expenses, and more) is stored only on your device in a local database. We do not operate servers that collect, store, or process your business data.

The only optional external integration is your Google account, and it is enabled only if you choose to connect it. This policy explains what information is involved, how it is used, and what control you have.

2. Information BlackCatPOS collects

BlackCatPOS does not collect or send data to any server operated by us. We cannot access information you enter in the software.

Information you enter manually is stored on your device and may include:

  • Business information: name, tax ID, address, phone number, and logo.
  • Sales, expenses, day-end reports, accounts, and payment methods.
  • Inventory, products, categories, batches, and prices.
  • Customers and employees (names, IDs, phone numbers, email addresses, PINs, and schedules).
  • System settings (taxes, currencies, permissions, and preferences).

The only information we may receive directly from you is contact information you voluntarily provide when purchasing an optional service. It is used only to provide that service.

3. How information is used

Information you enter is used exclusively to run the software on your own device:

  • Record and review sales, expenses, inventory, and customers.
  • Generate reports, day-end summaries, and Excel exports.
  • Manage access (employee PINs) and sessions on your local network (LAN).
  • Produce receipts and business tax documents.

None of this information leaves your device unless you choose to do so, for example by exporting a file or emailing a report (see section 5).

4. Google information and permissions

Google integration is entirely optional and is enabled only if you connect your account under Settings → Google Services. BlackCatPOS requests one Google permission: gmail.send, which allows the app to send email from your own account.

This permission does not allow BlackCatPOS to read your inbox, contacts, or existing emails; it can only send new email. BlackCatPOS does not access Drive, Calendar, Photos, or other Google services. The permission does not expose your profile, so the app may show connection status without displaying the account address.

5. Gmail and reports

The connected Google account is used only to send reports created in BlackCatPOS:

  • Manual sending: choose a recipient from a report and the app emails the Excel file.
  • Automatic reports: if enabled under Settings → Google Services → Automatic Reports, the day-end report is sent to recipients you configure.

Emails are sent from your own Gmail account only to recipients you specify. BlackCatPOS does not email third parties or send reports to us.

6. Information stored locally

Information is stored in the application data folder on your device (user://):

  • SQLite database containing your business information.
  • Image files for products and your business logo; images are not stored in the database.
  • Exported files, such as Excel reports.
  • Backups in .zip format when you create them under Settings → Backup/Restore.
  • Google connection information (obfuscated OAuth tokens; see section 8).

7. Google Drive and backups

BlackCatPOS does not currently upload or store anything in Google Drive or any cloud service. Backups are created locally as compressed files containing your database, product images, and logo. You choose where to save them, such as a USB drive, external disk, or your own Drive.

Cloud synchronization may be offered in a future version, but will never be enabled without your explicit consent, and this policy will be updated at that time.

8. OAuth token storage

When you connect Google, OAuth2 authorization takes place between BlackCatPOS and Google on your device. The long-lived refresh token is stored in your local database in obfuscated form (XOR + base64), not as plain text. The short-lived access token is cached to send email and refreshed automatically when it expires.

Tokens are never sent to BlackCatPOS servers or shared with third parties. Someone with physical access to your device and database could attempt to extract them, so we recommend protecting your device with a password.

9. We do not sell your data

BlackCatPOS does not sell, rent, transfer, or share your data with third parties. We have no advertising, do not track your usage, and do not monetize personal information.

We also do not use your contact details for advertising unless you initiate contact with us to purchase a service.

10. Disconnecting Google

You can revoke BlackCatPOS access to your Google account at any time:

  • In the app: go to Settings → Google Services and select Disconnect. The connection tokens and account information are removed from your local database.
  • In Google: visit your Google Account security settings, find third-party apps with account access, and revoke BlackCatPOS. This invalidates the Google-side tokens.

Disconnecting preserves your local business data; only Gmail report sending stops until you reconnect.

11. Deleting your information

Your information is on your device, so you have full control over its deletion:

  • Delete individual records (sales, customers, products, and more) in the relevant app sections.
  • Use the secure reset process in Settings to remove the local database.
  • Uninstall the app and delete its data folder to remove the database, images, and Google tokens.

If you purchased an optional service and want us to delete contact details or documents you shared with us, email geraldglitch@gmail.com. We will do so within 30 days.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when adding integrations. Changes will be published here with a new update date. For significant changes, such as adding a cloud service, we will notify you in the app before they take effect.
